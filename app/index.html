Platinum Roadmap

One playthrough, no mission replay, no New Game+. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so play it clean. Roughly 200 hours from first mission to platinum.

1 Story first

Chapters 1–4 + final mission · ~40–60 h

Play the story straight through. Make a manual save at the start of every mission — there is no replay. Before finishing Chapter 1, meditate on Lucia’s balcony at Jason’s house (see the missable warning). Keep a recent save before the Race Against Yago sequence in Chapter 3: it is time-sensitive.

2 Strangers & side strands

8 stranger strands + 5 character finales · ~25 h

Clear every “?” on the map. Stranger strands and the Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone finales unlock as the story progresses — sweep them between chapters instead of leaving them all for the end.

3 Activities & combat

Shooting range, races, stunts, heists · ~50 h

The grind lives here: gold all 42 Shooting Range challenges, 250 stunts, drone races at all 14 locations, 20 race wins, 5 bank vaults, one illegal operation per gang. Do the fun ones first; save the pure grinds for last.

4 Collectibles & cleanup

Cards, WAiNK, vehicles, exploration · ~60 h

Cards (3 sets), WAiNK at 100 locations, 100 vehicles discovered, 8 garages, all 52 areas of Leonida. When the last item ticks off, A Long Road pops — and the platinum follows.
